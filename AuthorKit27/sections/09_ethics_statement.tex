\section*{Ethical Statement}
\label{sec:ethics_statement}

\subsubsection*{Societal Impacts and Research Ethics}
This work investigates organizational behavior, power dynamics, and collective decision-making in multi-agent LLM systems. All experiments were conducted purely in \emph{in silico} synthetic multi-agent simulations; no human participants were involved, deceived, or subjected to workplace misconduct. As LLM-based autonomous agents are increasingly integrated into collaborative enterprise workflows, understanding how these models respond to supervisory authority, peer coordination, and organizational stress is essential for designing robust, transparent, and accountable multi-agent governance frameworks.
